Loan Calculator

Estimate a fixed monthly repayment for an installment loan before comparing lending terms. This is an illustrative calculation, not a lending offer.

How to use this tool

Enter the amount borrowed, annual nominal interest rate and term in years. We use the standard amortizing loan formula for equal monthly payments with monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12 and months = years × 12. At 0% interest, the principal is divided equally across all months. Real borrowing costs may include additional fees, insurance or variable interest.

Important note

This tool provides general estimates. Check the result before using it for important decisions.

Your entries are processed on your device; calculator inputs are not sent to a server.
