BMR & Daily Calorie Calculator

The Mifflin–St Jeor equation provides a general adult estimate of resting energy expenditure. Multiplying by an activity factor gives an approximate daily energy requirement.

How to use this tool

Enter age, weight, height, formula category and activity level. The formula uses (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) + a category coefficient (+5 or −161). Daily energy is the BMR estimate multiplied by the selected activity factor. This is a population-based estimate; medical conditions and individual differences can change needs.

Important note

This tool provides general estimates. Check the result before using it for important decisions.

Your entries are processed on your device; calculator inputs are not sent to a server.
