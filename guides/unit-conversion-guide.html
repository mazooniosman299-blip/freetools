Common Unit Conversions

Everyday conversions people search for every day. Use the formulas below or our free converter.

Length

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm
  • 1 foot = 30.48 cm
  • 1 meter = 3.2808 feet
  • 1 mile = 1.60934 km

Weight / Mass

  • 1 kg = 2.20462 pounds
  • 1 pound = 0.453592 kg
  • 1 ounce = 28.3495 grams

Temperature

  • °C to °F: (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • °F to °C: (°F − 32) × 5/9
  • 0°C = 32°F · 100°C = 212°F · 37°C ≈ 98.6°F

Volume

  • 1 liter ≈ 0.264 gallons (US)
  • 1 gallon (US) ≈ 3.785 liters
  • 1 cup (US) ≈ 236.6 ml
