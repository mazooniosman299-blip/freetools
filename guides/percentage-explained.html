How to Calculate Percentages

What is X% of Y?

Formula: (X ÷ 100) × Y

Example: 20% of 150 = 0.20 × 150 = 30

X is what percent of Y?

Formula: (X ÷ Y) × 100

Example: 30 is what % of 150? → (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%

Percentage change (increase or decrease)

Formula: ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100

Example: From 100 to 120 → ((120 − 100) ÷ 100) × 100 = +20%

Example: From 80 to 60 → ((60 − 80) ÷ 80) × 100 = −25%

Discount example

Item costs $80, 15% off → discount = 0.15 × 80 = $12 → final price = $68
