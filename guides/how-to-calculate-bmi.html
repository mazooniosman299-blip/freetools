How to Calculate BMI

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple number that estimates body fat based on height and weight. It is widely used by health organizations including the World Health Organization (WHO).

BMI Formula

Metric system:

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ [height (m)]²

Example: 70 kg and 1.75 m → 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.9

WHO Categories (Adults)

  • Underweight: BMI < 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25 – 29.9
  • Obesity class I: 30 – 34.9
  • Obesity class II: 35 – 39.9
  • Obesity class III: ≥ 40

Limitations

BMI does not measure body fat directly. Athletes with high muscle mass may have a high BMI without excess fat. It is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Always consult a healthcare professional for personal health advice.
