Discount Calculator

Know your savings before you buy. This calculator works with any currency when you enter the original price and discount percentage.

How to use this tool

Enter the original price and discount percentage from 0 to 100. Savings = original price × discount percentage ÷ 100. Final price = original price − savings. For multiple successive discounts, calculate them one at a time instead of adding their percentages. Taxes and fees are not included.

Important note

This tool provides general estimates. Check the result before using it for important decisions.

Your entries are processed on your device; calculator inputs are not sent to a server.
