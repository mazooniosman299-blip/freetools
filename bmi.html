BMI Calculator

BMI is a ratio of weight to height. It is widely used as a screening measure for adults, but it cannot measure body fat or diagnose a condition on its own.

How to use this tool

Enter weight in kilograms and height in centimeters. We calculate BMI using weight ÷ height², where height is expressed in meters. Common adult screening categories are below 18.5, 18.5 to below 25, 25 to below 30 and 30 or higher. These ranges are not intended for children, pregnancy or individual clinical assessment.

Important note

This tool provides general estimates. Check the result before using it for important decisions.

Your entries are processed on your device; calculator inputs are not sent to a server.
