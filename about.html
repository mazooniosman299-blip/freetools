About FreeTools

FreeTools is a free collection of practical online calculators and unit converters designed for everyday use.

All tools run entirely in your browser. We do not store your data or require an account.

The site supports both Arabic and English with a proper right-to-left layout when Arabic is selected.

Our goal is to provide fast, accurate, privacy-respecting tools that anyone can use without barriers.
