Text Case & Cleanup Tools

Convert text to uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case, trim lines and remove duplicate lines locally in your browser.

How to use this tool

Paste any text, choose a transformation and download or copy the result. Uppercase and lowercase respect Unicode scripts; case operations only alter letters that have letter case. Remove blank lines deletes whitespace-only lines, and duplicate removal preserves the first instance of every nonempty line.

Privacy and limitations

Text processing happens locally, using your browser. Input text is never sent to this site's server. Do not use Base64 as a way to protect secrets.
