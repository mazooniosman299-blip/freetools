Free QR Code Generator

Turn website links and text into downloadable QR codes. Generated locally on your device without sending your text to a QR service.

How does a QR code work?

A QR code encodes text or a URL into a grid of squares readable by a phone camera. Enter a complete link beginning with https:// or any short text, choose its size, generate and download a PNG. Test the QR with another device before printing. Some dense or lengthy data may be difficult to scan.

Privacy

QR generation runs in your browser using a bundled open-source library. Your entered text is not uploaded to our servers.
