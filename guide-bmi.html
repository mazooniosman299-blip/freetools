How to Calculate BMI

Body Mass Index (BMI) estimates body fat from height and weight. Used by the WHO.

Formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ [height (m)]²

Example: 70 kg, 1.75 m → 70 ÷ 3.0625 = 22.9

WHO Categories (Adults)

  • Underweight: < 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25 – 29.9
  • Obesity: ≥ 30

BMI is a screening tool only. Consult a doctor for health advice.
