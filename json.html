JSON Formatter & Validator

Format, minify and validate JSON online locally. Helpful errors, one-click copy and download. Your text never leaves your browser.

How to use this tool

Paste JSON, then choose Format for a readable indented version or Minify for compact output. Invalid JSON is reported without modifying the original input.

Privacy & limitations

Files and inputs are processed in your browser. The tool does not send your content to our servers. Review outputs before important use.
