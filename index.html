Common questions about our online tools

How do the tools work?

Choose a calculator or utility, enter your details and see a result on the same page. Conversions and calculations are performed by JavaScript in your browser. Many tools update automatically when you edit a value, so there is no registration or account setup.

Are results accurate?

Standard unit conversions use defined factors, while financial and health tools provide estimates based on their stated assumptions. Check important results independently. BMI and calorie tools cannot replace professional medical advice, and a loan quote may differ from your lender's actual terms.

Are uploads or payments required?

No registration or payment is required. Photos are resized and converted in your browser; JSON, text, QR and passwords are processed locally too. We do not send the content entered into our utility forms to our servers. Browser support for image formats and secure APIs may vary.
