Tip Calculator

Estimate the amount to pay when you want to leave a percentage tip and split a bill between friends or colleagues.

How to use this tool

Enter the bill before tip, choose the tip percentage and enter how many people are sharing. Tip = bill × tip rate ÷ 100. Total = bill + tip. Each person's share = total ÷ number of people. Use one consistent currency throughout; this calculator does not perform currency exchange.

Important note

This tool provides general estimates. Check the result before using it for important decisions.

Your entries are processed on your device; calculator inputs are not sent to a server.
